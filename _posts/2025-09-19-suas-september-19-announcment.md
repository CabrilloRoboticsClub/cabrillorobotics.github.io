Cabrillo College’s Robotics Club is excited to announce the debut of [**SkyHawk**](/skyhawk), our first Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV). This project marks a major step forward for the team as we expand into aerial robotics and tackle the challenges of autonomous flight.  
<br>
The [**Student Unmanned Aerial Systems (SUAS) Competition**](/suas), hosted annually in Maryland by RoboNation, brings together over 50 international teams to design and demonstrate autonomous aerial vehicles. Competition tasks mirror real-world applications and include autonomous navigation, object detection and classification, payload delivery, and mapping.  
<br>
The [**SkyHawk**](/skyhawk) represents Cabrillo Robotics’ first step into aerial autonomy, requiring expertise in avionics, flight dynamics, and computer vision. Through this project, students are gaining hands-on experience in cutting-edge technologies that power modern UAV systems.  
<br>
Cabrillo Robotics is eager to take on the challenges of SUAS 2026 and looks forward to representing Cabrillo College on the international stage.
