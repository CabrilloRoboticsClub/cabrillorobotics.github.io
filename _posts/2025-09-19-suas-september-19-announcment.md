Cabrillo College’s Robotics Club is excited to announce two major projects for the upcoming year: the debut of [**SkyHawk**](/skyhawk), our first Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV), and the continued refinement of [**LazerShark**](/lazershark), our first Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV). These projects mark a significant step forward for the team as we expand from underwater robotics into the skies, while also pushing the limits of our AUV capabilities.  
<br>
The [**Student Unmanned Aerial Systems (SUAS) Competition**](/suas), hosted annually in Maryland by RoboNation, brings together over 50 international teams to design and demonstrate autonomous aerial vehicles. Competition tasks mirror real-world applications and include autonomous navigation, object detection and classification, payload delivery, and mapping. The [**SkyHawk**](/skyhawk) represents Cabrillo Robotics’ first step into aerial robotics, requiring expertise in avionics, flight dynamics, and computer vision.  
<br>
At the same time, the team is continuing to develop the [**LazerShark**](/lazershark), the club’s first AUV, which made its debut at RoboSub 2025. Unlike tethered ROVs, AUVs must navigate independently, relying on an advanced suite of sensors and software to make decisions underwater without human intervention. For RoboSub 2026, LazerShark is being upgraded with enhanced computer vision, redesigned custom PCBs, and more robust autonomy algorithms to improve performance in complex mission tasks.  
<br>
These parallel projects reflect the growing capabilities of the Cabrillo Robotics Club and the dedication of its student engineers. By competing in both RoboSub and SUAS, the team is pushing into two of the most challenging fields in modern robotics—underwater and aerial autonomy. Cabrillo Robotics is eager to take on these challenges in 2026 and looks forward to representing Cabrillo College on the international stage.
